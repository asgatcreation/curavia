{% comment %} One sticker per page, 50 x 25 mm, black on white. Everything is in millimetres. A label is a physical object: it has to come out of the printer at the size the roll is, and a barcode scaled by a browser is a barcode that does not scan. There is no colour, because a thermal printer has none, and no web font, because the printer may be on a trolley with no network. The layout is a four-row stack rather than a grid: accession and barcode, the patient, the tube, and the time.
