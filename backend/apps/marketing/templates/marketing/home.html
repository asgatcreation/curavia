One system for your whole hospital.

Front desk, triage, consultation, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy, cashier and wards — every department in one place, handing patients to each other as it happens. When a doctor prescribes, the pharmacist on seat sees it instantly, with allergies, interactions and stock already checked.

This is a development build. Milestone 2 of the build plan ships the foundations: multi-hospital tenancy, staff accounts with mandatory two-factor authentication, roles and object-level permissions, and a hash-chained audit log. The marketing site and the hospital screens are built out in later milestones.

Built for Nigerian practice

Prices in naira, stored as whole kobo so nothing is ever lost to rounding. NAFDAC numbers, batches and expiry dates on every medicine. HMO and NHIA tariffs, pre-authorisation codes and claim batches. The national immunisation schedule. Times in West Africa Time. Verification against CAC registration, a state Ministry of Health facility licence and the medical director's MDCN number before a hospital goes live.

Privacy by construction

Each hospital's records are unreachable from any other, enforced in the database and proved by a test suite that deliberately attempts to cross the boundary. Every view and every change of a patient record is written to an append-only, hash-chained audit log. Curavia's own staff cannot open a hospital's records at all unless that hospital grants time-limited access — and everything done under that grant is recorded where the hospital can see it.
