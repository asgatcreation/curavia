Data Processing Agreement

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the hospital is the data controller and Curavia is the data processor. This page sets out what that means in practice.

Draft. The executable agreement, with the full schedules of sub-processors and security measures, is published at milestone 12 alongside the platform level. It is listed here now because the architecture was designed around it rather than the other way round, and because a hospital evaluating us will ask for it first.

Who decides what

The hospital decides what patient data is collected, why, how long it is kept and who may see it. Curavia processes that data only on the hospital's instructions, to provide the service, and for no purpose of its own. We do not sell data, we do not use patient records to train models, and we do not share them with anyone the hospital has not instructed us to share them with.

Access by Curavia staff

None by default. Support access to a hospital's records requires the hospital to grant it, to a named person, for a limited period, with a reason. It is logged into the hospital's own audit log and it expires automatically.

Sub-processors

The service depends on a database host, an object-storage provider, an email provider, an SMS provider, a payment processor and a video provider. Each is listed with its role and location in the schedule, and the hospital is told before one changes.

Breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach we notify the affected hospital without undue delay, with what we know and what we are doing, in time for the hospital to meet its own 72-hour obligation to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. We assist with that notification.

Retention, export and deletion

Medical-record retention periods are the hospital's to set, in its own settings. A hospital may export everything it holds at any time. On termination the hospital chooses: export and delete, or export and keep a read-only archive for an agreed period. We never delete a hospital's data without notice and an export.
