Privacy policy

Draft. The full policy is published at milestone 12. The architecture behind these statements is described in Security and privacy, and the controller/processor split in the Data Processing Agreement.

The public demonstration of Curavia contains synthetic data only. The patients, histories, results and bills in it are invented. No real patient information is used anywhere in the demonstration, the repository or the tests.

Patient records

When a hospital uses Curavia, the records in it belong to that hospital and are processed on its instructions. Patients exercise their rights — to see their record, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to ask for deletion — through the hospital, and the patient portal raises those requests directly, with a deadline the hospital can see.

What we collect about staff and visitors

For staff accounts: name, work email, phone, role and branch, plus the security record needed to keep the account safe — sign-in times, the device list, and the audit log of what was opened and changed. For visitors to this site: the minimum needed to serve the page and keep it available. We choose the most privacy-preserving option available to us and ask for nothing we do not need.

Where data is held

Records are held in a managed PostgreSQL database and, for files such as results and scans, in private object storage reachable only through short-lived signed links. There is no public URL for a medical file. Sensitive fields are encrypted in the database with keys held outside it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about data a hospital holds in Curavia, go to the hospital first as data controller. For questions about Curavia itself, contact @asgatcreation.
