Terms of service

Draft. The full terms are published at milestone 12, with the platform level and paid plans. What follows are the points that will not change.

Not a medical device

Curavia is hospital administration and record-keeping software. It is not a certified medical device and it does not diagnose. Its clinical decision support — the interaction, allergy, dose and duplicate-therapy checks shown to prescribers and pharmacists — is informational. A clinician remains responsible for every clinical decision, and the system records when a warning was overridden and the reason given.

The demonstration

The public demonstration holds synthetic data and is reset regularly. It is for evaluation. Do not enter real patient information into it.

Your data and your account

A hospital's records remain the hospital's. It may export everything at any time. A hospital whose subscription lapses becomes read-only after notice and a grace period; its data is not deleted without notice and the opportunity to export it. Accounts are personal — staff must not share logins, and two-factor authentication is required.

Availability

The current demonstration runs on free hosting and carries no availability commitment. Paid plans state theirs.

Ownership

Curavia is the work of Akanji Oluwaseun Gabriel. The source is published to be read, not licensed for use — see the LICENSE file in the repository.
